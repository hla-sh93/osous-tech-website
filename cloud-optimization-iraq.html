What is Cloud Optimization?

Cloud optimization is the strategic process of correctly allocating cloud resources to maximize performance and minimize costs. It ensures your business workloads receive precisely the right resources as application needs evolve, eliminating waste from over-provisioning while maintaining optimal performance.
